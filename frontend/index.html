        How DocuMind answers

        1. Add documents. PDF and TXT files are read page by page, so every passage keeps its page number.
        2. Split into chunks. Each document is cut into short chunks that hold one idea each, with a little overlap so no idea is cut in half.
        3. Match your question. Your question and every chunk become lists of numbers (embeddings). The chunks closest in meaning to your question are retrieved.
        4. Answer with sources. Only those chunks go to the language model. It must answer from them, cite them, and say so when the answer isn't there.
